Question no.4

IQR of the price attribute

IQR(Carseats$Price) #
## [1] 31

The Interquartile Range (IQR) calculates the middle 50% of the data. It is computed by finding the difference between the 75th percentile (Q3) and the 25th percentile (Q1) values. The maximum and minimum values are not considered in this calculation. To find the IQR, subtract Q1 from Q3: IQR = Q3 - Q1. For example, Q3 is 131 and Q1 is 100, then the IQR would be 131 - 100 = 31.
